Stride support

Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Stride is developed by Youcef Boukandoura. This policy describes the running-records app and its support pages.

Apple Health access

With your permission, Stride reads running workouts and Walking + Running Distance from Apple Health. This includes workout dates, duration, distance, workout source, indoor-workout information, manual-entry information, and associated distance samples. Stride uses this information to calculate running records, pace, and streaks and display your activity history.

Stride requests read access only. It does not add or modify Health data. You can manage access in Apple Health under your profile → Apps → Stride.

Your data stays on your device

Imported Health data and derived results are processed in the app’s memory on your device. Stride does not upload Health data to the developer or another server, sell it, or use it for advertising. Results are refreshed from Apple Health when you return to the app or refresh it. A local preference remembers whether the Health permission flow has been attempted; it does not store your workout history.

No accounts, ads, or tracking

Stride does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. Stride does not request your routes, precise location, heart rate, or contacts.

Control and deletion

Health records remain in Apple Health. Removing Stride does not delete those records. To delete or change a Health record, use Apple Health or its source app. To stop Stride from reading Health data, revoke its access in Apple Health. Your displayed results are cleared and reloaded on the next refresh.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives the email address and information you choose to send and uses them to respond and diagnose the issue. Please avoid sending unnecessary Health data. Email is handled by the email providers involved in sending and receiving your message.

TestFlight and platform services

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple handles installation, beta feedback, crash information, and other TestFlight services under Apple’s privacy policies. These services are separate from Stride’s on-device Health processing.

Support website

These static pages include no custom analytics or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary access information, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

The policy may change as the app evolves. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions or support requests, contact youcef.boukandoura@gmail.com.
