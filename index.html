Support

Need help? Email youcef.boukandoura@gmail.com. Include your iOS version, Stride version, and a description of the problem. Please avoid sending health data or screenshots containing personal information unless needed to explain the issue.

No runs showing?

In Apple Health, open your profile, choose Apps, then Stride. Allow read access to Workouts and Walking + Running Distance. Return to Stride and pull down to refresh. You need saved running workouts; a walking history alone does not create running records.

Why are some records missing?

Estimated splits require detailed distance samples. When those are unavailable, a workout that finishes between the target distance and 3% above it may qualify with its entire elapsed time. Stride never projects a longer run’s average pace into a shorter-distance record. Manually entered runs do not set records.

Why does my pace differ from Fitness?

Stride uses elapsed time, including pauses. Fitness or another source may show moving or active time. Estimated splits are for personal tracking and are not official race results.

How are streaks counted?

A weekly streak requires at least one running workout each local calendar week. The current unfinished week stays open until it ends. Daily streaks count consecutive days with at least one run.
